# curriculum_ladder.md (stable; edit only when the plan changes)

Statuses (TAUGHT, CURRENT, NEXT, FUTURE) live in weekly_status.md. Claude may use only TAUGHT and CURRENT units. "Needs" lists the units that must be TAUGHT or CURRENT first. "Stance" is formal (precise definitions, theorems used only as supplied) or informal (hand-waved; state no claim the teacher has not supplied). Definitions for units in the gold files are the ones in those files' Facts sheets. For new units the teacher supplies definitions.

## Ordered units
- U01 Fractions, patterns, expansion. Complex fractions, pattern practice, expansion patterns, true/false patterns, three-term products. Formal.
- U02 Linear equations in one variable. Basic, medium, with fractions, solving for an indicated variable. Needs U01. Formal.
- U03 Factoring. Canonical form k(ax + b), factoring out an expression, difference of squares, trinomials with a not 1 (several methods), mixed factoring. Needs U01. Formal.
- U04 Square roots. Simplifying, including harder simplification. Needs U01. Formal. Cube roots and higher roots are not yet introduced (see F1).
- U05 Quadratic equations. Solving by factoring and by completing the square, "paired factoring and solving." Needs U03, U04. Formal. Completing the square will be revisited in U16.
- U06 Rational equations. Reduce to linear or quadratic equations, extraneous solutions. Needs U02, U05. Formal. Say "multiply both sides by the same nonzero expression."
- U07 Coordinate plane. Ordered pairs, solution set, graph of an equation, graphing by plotting points. Needs U02. Formal.
- U08 Lines. Slope, y-intercept, slope-intercept form, point-slope form. Needs U07. Formal.
- U09 Systems of two linear equations. Solving by equating and by substitution, number of solutions. Needs U08. Formal.
- U10 Finishing linear equations. x-intercept, standard form (the name), elimination, parallel and perpendicular lines. Needs U09. Formal.
- U11 Geometry toolkit. Pythagorean theorem (teacher shows first), distance formula, midpoint formula. Needs U04, U08. Formal. Radical answers are natural here. Little on circles.
- U12 Linear inequalities. Solving, compound inequalities (and/or). Needs U02. Formal. Ask the teacher before using interval notation.
- U13 Graphing linear inequalities. Half-planes. Needs U08, U12. Formal.
- U14 Systems of linear inequalities. Graphing the solution region. Optional contextless linear programming. Needs U10, U13. Informal for linear programming (do not state that an optimum occurs at a vertex unless supplied).
- U15 Parabolas geometrically. A parabola as the set of points equidistant from a point and a line (use equal squared distances); quadratics have parabolas as graphs. Needs U05, U07, U11. Formal. No function notation; use "graph of an equation."
- U16 Vertex form. Revisits completing the square. Light graphical transformations, equations only. Needs U15. Informal for transformations.
- U17 Quadratic formula. Derived by completing the square, discriminant, Vieta's formulas. Needs U16. Formal. Irrational roots are expected.
- U18 Complex numbers. Arithmetic, complex roots of quadratics, conjugates. Needs U17. Formal.

## Middle block (order to be chosen by the teacher; each waits only on its Needs)
- M1 Absolute value equations and inequalities. Needs U12. Formal. Equations before inequalities.
- M2 Quadratic inequalities. Needs U12, U15, U17. Formal.
- M3 Radical equations. Squaring both sides, conjugation, extraneous roots, challenging algebra. Needs U04, U06, U17. Formal.
- M4 Line-parabola systems. By substitution. Needs U09, U15, U17. Formal.

## Later units
- U19 Polynomial arithmetic and exponent laws. Check with the teacher whether integer exponents are already covered. Needs U03. Formal.
- U20 Higher-degree polynomials. Division, synthetic substitution, factor and remainder theorems, constructing a polynomial with given roots, the fundamental theorem of algebra and its corollary for real polynomials (stated, not proved). Needs U18, U19. Informal for the fundamental theorem. Ask the teacher about the rational root theorem.
- U21 Functions and domain, introduced only as needed. Function notation, domain. Needs U07. Formal. The words "function" and "domain" stay banned until this unit is CURRENT or TAUGHT.
- U22 Rational functions. Solving equations first, then graphs. Needs U06, U20, U21. Informal for limits and asymptotes (describe behavior, claim no limit theorems).
- U23 Rational inequalities. Needs U22, M2. Formal.
- U24 Functions in depth. Domain, composition, inverses. Needs U21. Formal. Far in the future.
- F1 nth roots and rational exponents. Position to be chosen by the teacher. Needs U19.
